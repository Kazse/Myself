Gym guide

Beginner friendly

Mostly cables and light dumbbells. Start with a light weight, controlled reps and 60–90 seconds rest. Aim to finish each set feeling you could still do 2–3 good reps.

Quick safety note

You’ve mentioned significant calf pain when walking. Don’t push through pain or add painful standing/cardio movements. Keep leg work pain-free and check your lower-body plan with your physio.

Cable machine example · Image: Bells of Steel

Legs — machine-based, only if pain-free

Use seated machines so you can avoid unnecessary walking or standing.

Seated leg curl2 sets × 10–12 reps

Adjust the pad above your heels, curl slowly, then return under control.

Leg extension2 sets × 10–12 reps

Align your knee with the machine pivot. Extend without snapping your knees straight.

Seated hip abductor2 sets × 12–15 reps

Push the pads outward smoothly; avoid bouncing or forcing the range.

Back

Lat pulldown2 sets × 10–12 reps

Pull the bar towards your upper chest; keep your torso steady and avoid pulling behind your neck.

Seated cable row2 sets × 10–12 reps

Sit tall, pull the handle towards your lower ribs, and gently bring your shoulder blades together.

Straight-arm cable pulldown2 sets × 12 reps

With a slight bend in your elbows, bring the bar down towards your thighs without swinging.

Shoulders

Seated dumbbell shoulder press2 sets × 8–10 reps

Use light dumbbells; press up without arching your lower back. Skip if it causes discomfort.

Dumbbell lateral raise2 sets × 10–15 reps

Raise the weights out to the sides to about shoulder height, with soft elbows and no swinging.

Cable face pull2 sets × 12–15 reps

Set the cable around face height and pull towards your face, elbows out, keeping shoulders relaxed.

Biceps

Cable biceps curl2 sets × 10–12 reps

Keep elbows close to your sides and lower the handle slowly.

Seated dumbbell hammer curl2 sets × 10–12 reps

Keep palms facing each other and avoid rocking your body.

Triceps

Rope triceps pushdown2 sets × 10–12 reps

Keep elbows tucked in, straighten your arms, then return slowly.

Seated one-dumbbell overhead extension2 sets × 10–12 reps

Use a light weight and keep elbows pointing forwards. Skip if it irritates your shoulders.

Chest

Standing cable chest press2 sets × 10–12 reps

Set handles around chest height, stagger your stance only if comfortable, and press forward without shrugging.

Seated chest press machine2 sets × 10–12 reps

Set handles around mid-chest and press smoothly; keep your back against the pad.

Core

Seated cable Pallof press2 sets × 8–10 reps each side

With the cable at chest height, press hands forward and resist being rotated. Keep the weight light.

A simple weekly option: Monday back + biceps, Wednesday legs (only if pain-free) + core, Friday chest + shoulders + triceps. Rest between sessions. Begin with 1–2 sets if you’re new, and increase gradually. General guidance: NHS strength-training advice.

Food & recipes

Simple meals

Straightforward meals with everyday ingredients. Tap a recipe to see ingredients and how to make it. Portions are flexible; these are ideas, not strict diet rules.

Chicken, rice & veg bowl25 minutes · Lunch or dinner

Ingredients · 1 serving

  • 1 chicken breast or 150–200 g chicken pieces
  • 1 microwave rice portion
  • 1–2 handfuls frozen or fresh vegetables
  • 1 tsp oil, paprika, garlic granules, salt and pepper

How to make it

  1. Cut chicken into even pieces and season with paprika, garlic, pepper and a little salt.
  2. Heat oil in a pan. Cook chicken for about 6–10 minutes, turning, until piping hot and fully cooked through (no pink centre).
  3. Microwave the rice according to the packet instructions.
  4. Steam or microwave vegetables until tender, then serve everything in a bowl.

Swap chicken for tofu or beans if preferred.

Easy chicken burrito bowl20–25 minutes · Lunch or dinner

Ingredients · 1 serving

  • 150–200 g chicken breast
  • 1 microwave rice portion
  • ½ tin black beans or kidney beans, drained
  • Sweetcorn, chopped tomato and lettuce
  • ½ tsp paprika, ½ tsp cumin, squeeze of lime or lemon
  • Optional: a spoon of salsa or plain yoghurt

How to make it

  1. Season chicken with paprika, cumin and pepper.
  2. Pan-cook in a little oil until fully cooked through, turning regularly.
  3. Heat rice and beans according to their packet instructions.
  4. Add rice, beans, sweetcorn, tomato and lettuce to a bowl.
  5. Slice the chicken on top and finish with lime, salsa or yoghurt.
🥣Greek yoghurt, banana & oats5 minutes · Breakfast

Ingredients · 1 serving

  • 200 g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 banana
  • 30–40 g oats
  • Cinnamon or a few berries, optional

How to make it

  1. Spoon yoghurt into a bowl.
  2. Slice the banana over the top.
  3. Add oats and cinnamon or berries. Stir together or layer it.

For overnight oats, mix the ingredients in a lidded container and refrigerate overnight.

🌯Chicken & salad wrap10–15 minutes · Lunch

Ingredients · 1 serving

  • 1 large tortilla wrap
  • Cooked chicken pieces (leftovers are fine)
  • Lettuce, cucumber and tomato
  • 1 tbsp plain yoghurt or light mayo

How to make it

  1. Mix yoghurt or mayo with black pepper and a squeeze of lemon.
  2. Lay the salad and chicken down the centre of the wrap.
  3. Add the sauce, fold in the sides and roll tightly.
  4. Optional: toast seam-side down in a dry pan for 1–2 minutes.
🍝Quick tomato & tuna pasta15 minutes · Lunch or dinner

Ingredients · 1 serving

  • 75–90 g dry pasta
  • ½–1 tin tuna, drained
  • ½ tin chopped tomatoes
  • Garlic granules, black pepper and dried herbs
  • Optional: frozen peas or spinach

How to make it

  1. Boil pasta in water following the packet instructions, then drain.
  2. Warm chopped tomatoes in a pan with garlic, herbs and pepper for 5–7 minutes.
  3. Stir in tuna and peas or spinach until hot.
  4. Mix with the pasta and serve.
